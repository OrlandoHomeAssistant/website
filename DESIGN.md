# Home Assistant Orlando

Palette: lake blue #087BA6, deep lake #103746, sky #EDF6F8, paper #FFFFFF, citrus #FFAD51, ink #16323D.
Typography: Figtree variable 400–800 throughout, matching Home Assistant websites. Self-hosted Latin WOFF2 with system fallbacks; no remote font requests.
Layout: left-aligned editorial welcome paired with a custom Florida smart-home illustration. Full-width next-meetup ticket, followed by a compact resource workbench and Discord invitation.

Review: Avoid a generic dashboard/bento interpretation of smart homes. No invented community metrics or decorative graphs. The memorable element is a small Florida bungalow with a real light toggle; everything else supports joining or finding useful information. The bungalow is original SVG. The favicon, header, and gable use a simplified SVG rendition of the user-supplied Orlando fountain-house logo. Device light/dark preference controls the theme. Keep page buildless and third-party-script free.
